🧠 Did You Know?

🏔️ Home to K2

Pakistan boasts K2, the world's second-highest mountain at 8,611 meters, located in the Karakoram range. It's renowned for its challenging climbs and breathtaking vistas.

🌍 Fifth Most Populous

With over 220 million people, Pakistan ranks as the fifth most populous country globally, following China, India, the USA, and Indonesia.

🧪 Nobel Laureates

Two Pakistanis have won Nobel Prizes: Abdus Salam in Physics (1979) and Malala Yousafzai in Peace (2014), highlighting contributions to science and human rights.

🎨 Vibrant Truck Art

Pakistani truck art transforms vehicles into moving masterpieces, featuring intricate designs, poetry, and cultural motifs, reflecting regional identities and artistic expression.

👩‍⚖️ First Female PM

In 1988, Benazir Bhutto became the first female Prime Minister of a Muslim-majority country, serving two terms and leaving a lasting political legacy.

🛠️ Surgical Instrument Hub

Sialkot, Pakistan, is a global leader in surgical instrument manufacturing, supplying approximately 80% of the world's demand, showcasing industrial prowess.

🕌 Ancient Civilizations

Pakistan is home to ancient sites like Mohenjo-daro and Harappa, centers of the Indus Valley Civilization dating back over 5,000 years.

🌊 Gwadar Port

Gwadar, located on the Arabian Sea, hosts one of the world's largest deep-sea ports, playing a pivotal role in regional trade and the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor.

🌳 Mangrove Conservation

Pakistan's mangrove forests, especially near Karachi, are vital for coastal protection and biodiversity, with ongoing efforts to preserve and expand them.
