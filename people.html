🌟 Diversity of the People

Punjabis 🏞️

Largest ethnic group in Pakistan, mainly residing in Punjab province. Known for their hospitality, vibrant Bhangra dance, and hearty cuisine like butter chicken and lassi.

Pashtuns 🏔️

Residing mainly in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan, they follow a code called "Pashtunwali" emphasizing honor, hospitality, and bravery.

Sindhis 🌾

Native to Sindh province, known for their rich history tied to the Indus Valley Civilization. They celebrate traditional Ajrak patterns and Sindhi music.

Baloch ⛰️

Mostly found in Balochistan, Baloch people are proud of their tribal traditions, poetry, and resilience. Their hospitality is legendary in the desert regions.

Mohajirs 🕌

Descendants of migrants from India post-Partition, mostly residing in Karachi. They have contributed greatly to the country's urban development and education.

Gilgitis & Baltis 🏔️❄️

Inhabitants of Gilgit-Baltistan in the north, they are known for living among the world’s highest peaks. Their culture blends Tibetan and Central Asian influences.

Chitrali 🐐

Native to Chitral in KPK, known for their Kalash culture, colorful festivals, and unique Indo-Aryan language and traditions.

Kashmiris 🌲

Living in Azad Jammu and Kashmir, they are known for their beautiful traditional dress, handicrafts like shawls, and Wazwan cuisine.

Hazaras 🐴

Mainly in Quetta, Hazaras are a Persian-speaking ethnic group with Mongol descent, known for their peaceful community and resilience.
