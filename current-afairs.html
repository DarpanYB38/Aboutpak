🔥 Nushki Oil Tanker Explosion

Date: April 28, 2025

An oil tanker explosion in Nushki, Balochistan, resulted in 3 deaths and over 60 injuries. The incident occurred when a leaking tanker caught fire, leading to a massive blast. Emergency response teams are investigating the cause of the leak and assessing safety protocols in the region.

🌡️ Intense Heatwave Hits Pakistan

Date: May 1, 2025

Pakistan is experiencing an extreme heatwave, with temperatures soaring up to 48°C (118.4°F) in Sibi, Balochistan. The heatwave has led to 19 fatalities and widespread health concerns. Authorities are urging citizens to take precautions and stay hydrated.

🏛️ Babarloi Sit-In Against Canal Project

Date: April 29, 2025

Protests erupted in Babarloi, Sindh, against the federal government's plan to construct six new canals on the Indus River. Lawyers, nationalist groups, and civil society organizations are demanding the cancellation of the project, citing threats to water resources and the environment.

🚀 Pakistan Joins China's Chang’e-8 Lunar Mission

Date: February 5, 2025

Pakistan's Space and Upper Atmosphere Research Commission (SUPARCO) signed a Memorandum of Understanding with China's National Space Administration (CNSA) to include Pakistan’s first lunar rover in China's Chang’e-8 mission, scheduled for 2028. The mission aims to explore the Moon’s south pole.
